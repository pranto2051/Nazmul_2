# Design: Liquid Glass Research Portfolio

## Principles
1. **Clinical clarity before spectacle.** Figures, citations, statuses, and methods remain readable over every material.
2. **Evidence has visual weight.** Research imagery and publication metadata outrank decorative effects.
3. **Depth communicates structure.** Glass separates navigation and interactive tools; editorial content stays calm.

## Type
- Display: Space Grotesk, 700/600, 48–88px desktop, 42–58px mobile.
- Body and abstracts: Newsreader, 18–22px, 1.7 line height, max 72ch.
- Interface: Space Grotesk, 13–16px, normal tracking.

## Critique
The design avoids generic glassmorphism by limiting expensive blur, using an editorial serif, varying bento scale, avoiding rainbow text, and reserving refraction for the portrait and lightbox. Ambient motion stops after the entry sequence; interaction creates the remaining movement.
